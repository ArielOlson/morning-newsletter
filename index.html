SETTINGS

Your morning, your way.

This edition is made for Ariel in New York.

Apple or Google Calendar

Add an Apple public iCal link, a Google public calendar link, or an HTTPS .ics address to urls in config/calendar.local.json. Google links refresh directly when the calendar has a readable feed. Your connected account can provide a fallback during the daily scan.

Your link stays out of GitHub and out of the webpage. Calendar event details appear in your edition.

Leave a note for future you

Use Add a reminder on your phone or computer. On the website, reminders are encrypted and saved in that browser, carry into later daily editions, and can be edited or deleted from the same form. They do not sync between devices or enter published past editions; clearing browser data removes them. For a reminder shared across your devices, tell me “add this to the newsletter” with a date.

Delivery updates

The daily task reads shipping emails from your connected Gmail accounts. It saves package status, estimated dates, and tracking links. The page shows the scan time; Refresh edition rebuilds the page using the latest saved email and connected-calendar scan.

Fresh before five

Daily refresh is planned for 4:10 a.m. New York time. The local version needs your Mac awake and Codex running. After each refresh, the task pushes the finished edition to GitHub and verifies the published website.
